Ola Melon

Gentle animated stories for little ones, ages 1 to 8.

Ola Melon makes short, calm YouTube videos about Pip the bunny and the Sunny Meadow Friends. Each story teaches one small idea: sharing, saying sorry, trying again, going to sleep.
