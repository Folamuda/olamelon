Privacy Policy

Ola Melon Shorts Uploader · Last updated 29 September 2026

What this app is

Ola Melon Shorts Uploader is a private, single-user tool that publishes videos from the Ola Melon computer to the Ola Melon YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube scope to upload videos, set their title, description, tags and privacy status, and read basic channel information to confirm it is signed in to the correct channel. It does not read comments, subscriber lists, analytics, or any other viewer data.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a local file, and is used solely to talk to the YouTube API. No data is sent to any third party, no data is sold, and nothing is stored on a server.

Viewers of the channel

This app collects no information about people who watch Ola Melon videos. All Ola Melon videos are marked "made for kids" and follow YouTube's policies for children's content.

Deleting your data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored credentials.
